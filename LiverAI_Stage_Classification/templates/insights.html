Methodology and limitations

  • Preprocessing uses median imputation for numeric columns and most-frequent imputation plus one-hot encoding for categorical columns.
  • The classifier is a Random Forest with class balancing and a stratified train/test split.
  • The Status field is excluded from model inputs to reduce the risk of outcome leakage.
  • Exact duplicate rows are removed before splitting, but repeated patients or other dataset leakage may still be possible if provenance is unclear.
  • High test performance does not establish generalization to new populations or clinical usefulness.
